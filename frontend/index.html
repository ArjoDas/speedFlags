speedFlags: a free world flag quiz

Identify country flags by typing their names. Choose a suggestion with the arrow keys or pointer, then press Enter to submit.

Daily Challenge offers 30 flags and one attempt each day. Wrong answers and skips each add five seconds to your time. Finish the challenge to share your result grid, adjusted time, and score.

Race the clock in Timed mode, or learn at your own pace with untimed Practice. Review your answers and practice missed flags after a round.

Play at speedflags.win or speedflags.arjodas.com.
